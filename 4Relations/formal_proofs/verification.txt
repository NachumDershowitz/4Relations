The combined theorem is closed under the global context: it has no axioms
or unproved assumptions. All imported libraries are Rocq's Stdlib.

The theorem proves the four individual forward well-foundedness claims,
the three stronger interaction inclusions, conditions (P0), (P1), (Q2),
the p-to-q-to-p cycle, an explicit infinite alternating R-chain, and
failure of forward well-foundedness of R.

The standalone source also proves countability of the carrier and the
nilpotency bounds A^2 = C^2 = empty and D^3 = empty.

through ten points is established separately in the existing search
