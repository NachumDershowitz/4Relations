Four Relations companion archive, version 1.0.0

This release contains the current paper and editable source, its enlarged
vector figure, the Rocq formal proof of the infinite counterexample,
and the finite-search encoders, tables, final SMT inputs/outputs, and
coverage checks.

Verified results:
- Rocq 9.1.1 compilation and independent kernel check pass.
- The combined Rocq theorem is closed under the global context.
- The finite-coverage audit passes: 150 nine-point cases and 572 terminal
  ten-point obligations, with no unresolved cases.
- The two final SMT inputs regenerate byte for byte from the archived
  generator, with matching SHA-256 hashes.

The finite audit checks coverage and saved solver results; it does not
independently check Z3 proof certificates. The Rocq formalization covers
the explicit infinite counterexample.

Start with README.md. The paper is paper/Four_Well_Founded_Relations.pdf.
